Trick library

Nine common ways shops nudge you to spend more. Learn the tell, then practice spotting it.

🏷️

Fake “was” price

The shop raises the price for a while, then crosses it out and calls the old number a discount.

Spot itYou never saw that crossed-out price before.

➕

Stacked discounts

30% off plus an extra 20% sounds like 50%. The second one applies to the price left.

Spot itTwo percentages in one banner.

🎟️

Minimum-spend vouchers

A voucher can push you to buy more than planned just to unlock a small saving.

Spot it“Min. spend” in small print.

💸

Capped cashback

Big cashback percentages often have a low maximum and arrive later as points.

Spot it“Up to”, “max”, or “in points”.

🚚

Hidden fees

Delivery, service, and admin fees appear at the last step and can eat the whole discount.

Spot itThe total changes at checkout.

⏱️

Countdown pressure

A ticking clock makes you rush. Many of these timers simply restart.

Spot itA timer plus “only 2 left!”.

📦

Shrinking packs

The price stays the same, but the pack gets smaller. Only the price per 100 g tells the truth.

Spot itTwo similar packs, different sizes.

🔝

“Up to 70% off”

Only a few items get the biggest discount. Most of the shop may be 10% off or less.

Spot itThe words “up to”.

🧾

VAT surprise

Prices shown without tax look cheaper. VAT is added at the end.

Spot it“Excl. tax” near the price.
